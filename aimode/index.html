Set up "Google AI Mode" on your phone

Follow the steps for your phone. When you are done, searches you type into Chrome's address bar will open in Google's AI Mode, which gives you an AI-written answer with links below it.

This page does not see your searches. It only tells Chrome which web address to use. After these steps, your searches go straight to Google.

Android phone or tablet

  1. You are already on this page. Good.
  2. Open a new tab and search for anything on Google (for example, weather). Don't skip this step.
  3. Tap the three dots ⋮ in the top right corner, then Settings, then Search engine.
  4. Under Recently visited, tap Google AI Mode.
  5. Google AI Mode should now have a filled-in circle next to it. Take a screenshot of this screen.

iPhone or iPad

  1. You are already on this page. Good.
  2. Open a new tab and search for anything on Google (for example, weather). Don't skip this step.
  3. Tap the three dots ⋮ in the bottom right corner, then Settings, then Search engine.
  4. Under Recently visited, tap Google AI Mode.
  5. Google AI Mode should now have a checkmark next to it. Take a screenshot of this screen.

Check that it worked

  1. Open a new tab.
  2. In the address bar, type how does photosynthesis work and press Go.
  3. You should land on a page that says AI Mode at the top and gives you a written answer, with links below it.

Using a computer?

The steps for a computer are different. Please follow the computer instructions in the survey.
