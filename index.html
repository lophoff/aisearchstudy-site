Information and AI Settings in Search Engines

This site belongs to a research study at Princeton University about how people search for information online.

If you are taking part, please use the link in your survey to set up Chrome.

Set up "" on your phone

Follow the steps for your phone. When you are done,

This page does not see your searches. It only tells Chrome which web address to use. After these steps, your searches go straight to Google.

Android phone or tablet

  1. You are already on this page in Chrome. Good.
  2. Wait a few seconds for this page to finish loading. Then tap this button once, and come back to this page afterwards (don't skip this step): Search once on Google
  3. Tap the three dots ⋮ in the top right corner, then Settings, then Search engine.
  4. Under Recently visited, tap .
  5. should now have a filled-in circle next to it. Take a screenshot of this screen.

iPhone or iPad

  1. You are already on this page in Chrome. Good.
  2. Wait a few seconds for this page to finish loading. Then tap this button once, and come back to this page afterwards (don't skip this step): Search once on Google
  3. Tap the three dots ⋮ in the bottom right corner, then Settings, then Search engine.
  4. Under Recently visited, tap .
  5. should now have a checkmark next to it. Take a screenshot of this screen.
